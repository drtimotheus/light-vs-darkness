How to play (solo)

  • Win: demolish 5 Strongholds. Lose: your Life (❤) hits 0.
  • Your turn: tap the gold button to move through Ready → Draw → Anoint → Main → Attack → End. The bar always tells you what you can do now.
  • Anointing is your power. Play one Anointing card a turn, then tap it to add its color to your pool. The pool empties at the end of your turn.
  • Costs: a letter is one anointing of that color (R+2 = 1 Red and 2 of any color). Glowing cards are playable, and the game taps your Anointing cards to pay. Tap a grey card to see why it can’t be played.
  • Card effects run step by step. When a card needs a choice, the board dims and valid targets glow. Tap to choose, then Confirm. You can Cancel before anything is spent.
  • Attack: in the Attack phase, tap a glowing Living Truth, then a Stronghold. Damage stays on Strongholds between turns.
  • Darkness: at the end of Round 4, and then every even round, a Stronghold rises and every Stronghold attacks you one at a time. Play Reactions, then tap Living Truths to block. Leftover damage hits your Life.
  • Mentor: your Mentor gives a passive bonus and transforms into a 7/7 Living Truth when you play a Transform card.
  • Tap the i on any card for full details. Tap Log for the full history.
